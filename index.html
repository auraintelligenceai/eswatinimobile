Security & Hardening Controls

The infrastructure layer beneath e-Mali — encryption, least privilege, immutable vaults and auditable operations.

🔒

Encrypted backups

AES-256 encrypted archives with SHA-256 integrity manifests, generated by a hardened, fail-closed backup routine.

🛡

Least privilege

Administrative operations are restricted to the emaliadmins group through a curated sudoers policy.

🗁

Immutable vault

Archives are locked with the immutable attribute and pruned by a count-and-size retention policy. No silent overwrites.

🔗

Transport security

TLS setup for the portal with a staged certificate and key, keeping the delivery channel confidential in transit.

📜

Auditable logs

Rotated operational logs with a no-secrets scan, so activity is traceable without leaking sensitive values.

⚙

Verified restores

Restore tests prove backups are usable — integrity is checked, not assumed, before any archive is trusted.

Platform

Hosted on a Kali/Debian Linux laboratory virtual machine, served by Apache under the emali virtual host.
